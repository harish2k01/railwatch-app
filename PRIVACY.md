# RailWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

RailWatch is a personal train-travel planner for Android, developed by Harish T. This policy explains what
the app does with your information. In short: **everything stays on your phone.**

## What RailWatch stores

Your journeys, routines, time off, settings, ticket PDFs and the reminder inbox are stored only in
the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them. RailWatch has no account, no
server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What leaves your phone

RailWatch connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **PNR check.** When you tap Check PNR, the app sends the PNR and the code you type to Indian
  Railways' public enquiry service (indianrail.gov.in) and shows the reply. Nothing else is sent.
- **Ticket reading.** Text and QR codes on tickets are read on the phone by Google Play services. The
  ticket is not uploaded.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Booking, Tatkal, PNR and cancellation reminders |
| Alarms & reminders | So reminders arrive on the minute a booking window opens |
| Calendar (optional) | Only if you turn on Phone Calendar, to add your trips to a calendar you pick |
| Internet | PNR checks only |
| Run at startup | To restore reminders after the phone restarts |

## Backups

**Save Backup** writes one file to a place you choose, such as Drive or Files. That file holds your
plans and ticket PDFs. Keep it somewhere private.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling RailWatch, or clearing its storage in the phone's settings, deletes everything it
stored. Events it added to your calendar are removed when you turn Phone Calendar off.

## Children

RailWatch is not aimed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published with each app release and on the project's GitHub page.

## Disclaimer

RailWatch is an independent, personal project. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to
Indian Railways, IRCTC, CRIS or any railway authority**. Railway names are used only to describe the
trips you plan. Bookings are made by you, on IRCTC. PNR status comes from Indian Railways' public
enquiry service as-is; always confirm important details with the official sources.
